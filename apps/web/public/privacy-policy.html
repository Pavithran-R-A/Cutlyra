CUTLYRA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Summary: Cutlyra is a local-first Android video editor. The v0.1.0 app does not require an account, does not include ads or analytics, and does not upload your editing media, projects, or captions to Cutlyra servers.

Data Cutlyra accesses

When you choose media, Cutlyra accesses only the photos, videos, or audio files you select through Android system pickers. Selected media is copied into Cutlyra's app-private storage so editing, previews, captions, and export can work locally.

If you choose camera capture, Cutlyra requests camera access only for that user-initiated capture. The Android v0.1.0 app does not request broad photo/video library permissions, microphone permission, location, contacts, SMS, call-log, or phone permissions.

Collection, use, and sharing

Cutlyra does not transmit your projects, imported media, captions, usage activity, advertising identifiers, contacts, or location to Cutlyra servers. Cutlyra does not sell user data and does not share user data for advertising or analytics.

Cutlyra contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, telemetry service, account system, or cloud-sync service in the Android v0.1.0 release.

On-device captions

Automatic captions use a bundled whisper.cpp runtime and model locally on supported Android devices. Media is decoded and transcribed on-device; Cutlyra does not send audio or video to a remote speech-recognition service.

Secure handling

Imported working media and project data are kept in app-private/on-device storage. Android cloud backup is disabled for Cutlyra's app data. Cutlyra uses Android system pickers and narrowly scoped file-sharing configuration instead of requesting broad storage access.

Retention and deletion

Projects and imported working media remain on the device until you delete the project, clear Cutlyra's app data, or uninstall the app. Exported videos remain wherever you choose to save or share them and are under your control.

Cutlyra has no server-side user account, so there is no Cutlyra cloud account or server-side account data to delete.

Third-party components

Cutlyra uses open-source software libraries for its app shell, editing engine, media processing, and on-device captions. They run as part of the app. Cutlyra does not integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the Android v0.1.0 release.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Cutlyra's data practices or features change. The date at the top identifies the current revision.

Privacy inquiries

Cutlyra is the app covered by this policy. Privacy inquiries must be sent to the verified developer/support email published on Cutlyra's official Google Play listing. The publisher must keep that contact active and accessible wherever this policy is deployed.
